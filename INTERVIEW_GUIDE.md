# Interview Guide

## Tell me about the project
I developed a Power BI sales analytics dashboard using transactional plant-sales data. I prepared the source data, built a star-schema model, created DAX measures for sales, gross profit, YTD, PYTD and YTD versus PYTD, and created interactive visuals for country, product, month and account analysis.

## What did you personally do?
I handled data inspection, validation, modeling, DAX calculations, dashboard development and business interpretation.

## Why Power BI?
It combines data preparation, modeling, DAX and interactive visualization in one analytics platform.

## What is the fact table?
The fact table contains measurable business transactions such as sales, quantity and COGS.

## What is a dimension?
A dimension provides descriptive context such as product, account, country or date.

## Why a star schema?
It makes the model easier to understand, filter and maintain and generally supports efficient analytical queries.

## What is YTD?
Year to Date: performance from the beginning of the selected year through the selected/current date.

## What is PYTD?
Previous Year to Date: the comparable period in the previous year.

## Why a waterfall chart?
It helps explain the contributors to a positive or negative change rather than only showing the final KPI.

## What would you improve?
I would add rolling 12-month trends, customer profitability, product contribution, forecast, drill-through pages, row-level security and performance optimization.
